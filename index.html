Information about Tapasthali

Our story

Founded in 2048 BS (1991–92 AD), Tapasthali Briddha Ashram began as a small shelter for elderly women.

We are a non-governmental, non-profit organization.

Who we serve

Elderly women from all walks of life — widows, those abandoned by families, and women without financial support.

We also welcome women who wish to spend their later years in a spiritual environment.

📅 Established in 2048 BS — 1991/92 AD

For over three decades, Tapasthali has been a pillar of support for elderly women.
